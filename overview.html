    Packages in use
    Where each coach's running packages stand
    clients · open a coach for the names

    Sessions are counted against packages in order: a package takes sessions until it is used up, and the sessions after that belong to the next package, whenever it is paid. Sessions left = all the client's paid sessions not yet trained. Won't finish in time = at the client's pace over the last 4 weeks, more than one session will be left when the last package ends. Training beyond what's paid = sessions in the last 31 days after every paid session was used (usually a renewal not yet logged). Using sessions after the end date = sessions in the last 31 days taken from a package past its end date. Nearly used up = 2 sessions or fewer left. Not linked = the client's name on the sheet is not matched yet (see Name check).

    Share of the team
    Each coach's share of the active clients, the sessions and the revenue
    active clients, nowsessionsrevenueright: revenue share ÷ client share

    Active clients = clients with a running package today, by the coach on the Clients tab. Sessions and revenue = the coach sheets for the period picked. A coach with a quarter of the clients should bring in about a quarter of the revenue: 1.0× is the team's average. Below 1.0× means each of their clients trains less often or at a lower rate than the team's; the gap between the blue and orange bars says which. New coaches, leave and handoffs move these figures, so read them with that in mind.
